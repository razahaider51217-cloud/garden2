Why people garden

More than just plants

Gardening is a rewarding hobby that many people say helps them feel calmer, more active, and more connected to where their food comes from. While outcomes vary from person to person, the act of planting, watering, and watching things grow can be a meaningful daily routine.

  • Fresh, home-grown herbs, fruits, and vegetables
  • Light physical activity and time outdoors
  • A relaxing, screen-free daily ritual
